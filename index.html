Our services

Everything we haul away

If two people can carry it and it fits in the truck, it's going. Residential, commercial and rental turnovers across St. George and the surrounding towns.

Furniture Removal

Couches, sectionals, mattresses, recliners, desks and office chairs carried out of any room, up or down stairs, without a mark on your walls.

Appliance Hauling

Refrigerators, washers, dryers, ranges, water heaters and freezers disconnected from the curb, removed, and taken to certified metal recycling.

Garage & Home Cleanouts

Full garages, sheds, basements and storage units emptied in one visit. Tell us what stays, and the rest leaves with us.

Estate & Rental Turnovers

Discreet, respectful clearing of estates, foreclosures and post-tenant units for families, realtors and property managers on a deadline.

Construction Debris

Drywall, lumber, tile, roofing tear-off, fencing and remodel waste loaded and hauled — a faster, cheaper option than parking a dumpster on the job.

Yard Waste & Green Debris

Branches, palm trimmings, rock, sod, pavers and desert landscaping tear-out cleared and hauled to the right disposal site.

Hot Tub & Shed Demolition

Hot tubs, above-ground pools, swing sets and backyard sheds dismantled on site and carried out piece by piece — slab left clean.

Commercial & Office Cleanouts

Retail fixtures, cubicles, restaurant equipment and e-waste removed after hours so your doors open on time the next morning.

What we can't take: wet paint, solvents, asbestos, ammunition and other hazardous waste. Ask us and we'll point you to the right Washington County drop-off.

How it works

Three steps, one visit

Most jobs are booked, hauled and swept inside 48 hours.

  1. 1

    Send us the pile

    Fill out the quote form with a short description — or run the estimate tool below. A real person from the crew replies with a price range the same day.

  2. 2

    Lock in your window

    Pick a morning or afternoon window that works. We text when we're on the way, and we show up in a marked truck, on time.

  3. 3

    We load, haul and sweep

    You confirm the on-site price before we start. We do all the lifting, sweep the space, and route everything to recycling, donation or the transfer station.
